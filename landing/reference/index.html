The house on Mars has a tenant.

Where you are

A habitat that looks like someone's home. Kitchen, greenhouse, a workshop with the lights half on. The Artemis III is on the pad behind it.

What hunts you

It can't see well. It listens. Footsteps, doors, dropped tools, and your breathing all tell it where you are.

How you talk

You and your crewmate share a breath code. A sniff is 1. A puff is 2. Every word costs noise, so you get three.

say less. live longer.

Try the breath code.

Press 1 for a sniff and 2 for a puff, or tap the buttons. Pause 0.8 seconds to lock a letter, 2 seconds to lock a word. Watch the noise meter: past the red line, it hears you.

noise
Try: 1 (E), 2 (T), 11 (A), 22 (N).

Letter table

Common letters get the shortest codes. 1 = sniff, 2 = puff.

It remembers where it heard you.

The hunter is trained with reinforcement learning. It keeps a belief map of the house. Every sound raises the odds of a room, and the odds fade as time passes.

Stay quiet and it widens its search. Get loud and it commits.

Six rooms between you and the ship.

  • Kitchen. The fuse box hides behind the pantry. Your crewmate reads the order, you flip the switches.
  • Greenhouse. Glass floors and loud leaves. Good place to lose it, if you stay still.
  • Workshop. Tools that clatter when dropped. Useful as a lure.
  • Med bay. Locked until you find the code. Your crewmate has half of it.
  • Cold storage. A door that seals from outside. Two consoles, one shared word, ten seconds.
  • Launch pad. Punch in the code with your own breath. Every digit is noise.
